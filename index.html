FOGVALE

CHAPTER ONE · THE STABLE DOOR

The letter reached you eleven days ago, sealed in grey wax. It was signed by Elder Maren of Brookhollow, a village you had to search two maps to find.

It said only: "Come quickly. Bring no one. Trust no one on the road."

Now the last hill falls away behind you and the fog thins ahead. You can see exactly as far as your own two eyes allow — no further. Tap the land to walk it. Tap people to speak. What you learn from one mouth, you may ask of another.

HOW TO PLAY

Walk — tap any ground you can see or remember. Trees, walls and mountains block your sight; the world stays dark until you've laid eyes on it.

Talk — tap a person. Conversation runs on topics: words you learn from one person can be asked of everyone else. Listen for names.

Earn — walk over wortleaf herbs (small white-flowered sprigs) and bluecap mushrooms to gather them. Shops buy them — and pay far better the further the town is from where things grow.

Night — when night falls your sight closes to a few steps. A torch helps. A lantern helps more. Both are for sale... somewhere.

Fight — tap a creature beside you to strike it. A sword bites deeper; armour from the Ironvale forge turns teeth. If the fog takes you, you wake at the seer's door, lighter of purse. And mind: not everything that glows in the dark is your enemy — think before you swing.

The unseen — some of what you do is measured, though no number will ever show it. A seer lives at the south edge of Brookhollow, if you want a mirror.
